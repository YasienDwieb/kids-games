Kids Zone is a friendly, growing collection of simple, colorful games made just for young children — from toddlers to early grade-schoolers. No clutter, no confusing menus, just big buttons, happy sounds, and games that kids can play on their own.

Every game is designed to be picked up in seconds and enjoyed without reading: each game says what to do out loud, and a friendly helping hand shows the way if your child gets stuck. Cheerful 3D clay-toy pictures, gentle sound effects, and forgiving gameplay keep little ones smiling while they learn and play. And we add new games over time, so there's always something fresh to discover.

🦉 MEET MARIAM & ANAS, STARS & LEVELS

Your child picks a buddy, Mariam or Anas, who cheers them on in every game — tap them and they hop and cheer. A grown-up can even give the buddy a special name. Every win earns stars, stars raise your child's level, and each new level unlocks a hat or pair of glasses to dress their buddy up in. Stickers fill a sticker book along the way, and big moments end with a happy "LEVEL UP!" party.

🧭 A GUIDED JOURNEY, IF YOU WANT ONE

Prefer a hands-off option? Switch on the Guided Journey and Kids Zone gently leads your child from one bite-sized activity to the next — set against a calm scene that grows as they play. You choose which games are included.

👪 A CORNER FOR GROWN-UPS

See how long your child played each day this week, and the time and stars for every game. Set an optional daily play limit: when time's up, their buddy takes a gentle "recharge" break with a few stretches instead of a sudden stop.
